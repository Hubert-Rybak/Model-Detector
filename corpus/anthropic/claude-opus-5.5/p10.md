Pasta aglio e olio is the classic answer here. It's basically pasta, garlic and olive oil, and it takes about 15 minutes.

**Garlic and Olive Oil Pasta** (serves 2; double it for 4)

**Ingredients**
- 8 oz (225 g) spaghetti, or whatever pasta you have
- 4–6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- ¼ cup (60 ml) olive oil
- ¼–½ tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt
- Optional: grated Parmesan, chopped parsley, a squeeze of lemon

**Steps**
1. **Cook the pasta** in well-salted boiling water until it's about a minute short of al dente. Before draining, scoop out a cup of the pasta water.
2. **Make the sauce while the pasta boils.** Put the oil and garlic in a large skillet while it's still cold, then set it over medium-low heat. Stir now and then until the garlic is pale golden, 3–5 minutes. Don't let it brown or it'll turn bitter. Stir in the pepper flakes and take the pan off the heat.
3. **Toss it together.** Add the drained pasta and about ½ cup of the pasta water to the skillet. Toss hard over medium heat for 1–2 minutes, until the oil and water come together into a glossy sauce that coats the noodles. If it looks dry, add more water.
4. **Finish** with salt to taste, plus Parmesan, parsley or lemon if you have any.

**Easy variations, depending on what you have**
- **Canned tomatoes:** Once the garlic is golden, add a 14 oz (400 g) can of crushed or diced tomatoes and simmer about 10 minutes for a quick red sauce. For this version, start the sauce when you put the water on.
- **Richer:** Stir in a tablespoon of butter and a handful of Parmesan at the end.
- **Protein:** Add canned tuna or leftover chicken, or top it with a fried egg.
- **Veg:** Drop frozen peas or a few handfuls of spinach into the pasta pot for the last minute of cooking.
