Here's one that takes about 15 minutes and leans on stuff almost everyone has: pasta, garlic, olive oil (or butter), and some kind of hard cheese.

## Garlic Butter Pasta with Chili and Parmesan

**Serves 2 | ~15 minutes**

**You'll need:**
- 200g (about half a box) spaghetti or whatever pasta you have
- 3–4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter (or just more olive oil)
- Pinch of chili flakes (optional)
- A big handful of grated parmesan, pecorino, or honestly any hard cheese
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional: squeeze of lemon, chopped parsley, or a fried egg on top

**Method:**

1. **Boil the pasta** in well-salted water. Before draining, scoop out about a cup of the starchy cooking water. This is the key to a sauce that actually clings instead of pooling at the bottom.

2. **While it cooks**, warm the olive oil and butter in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook gently for 2–3 minutes until fragrant and just barely golden. Don't let it brown or it turns bitter.

3. **Drain the pasta** and tip it straight into the garlic pan. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water (start with 1/4 cup) and toss over the heat for a minute.

4. **Kill the heat**, add the cheese and a lot of black pepper, and keep tossing. Add more pasta water a splash at a time until it looks glossy and saucy rather than dry or clumpy.

5. Taste, adjust salt, and finish with lemon or parsley if you've got them.

## Easy upgrades depending on what's in the fridge

- **Half a can of tomatoes** stirred into the garlic for 5 minutes turns it into a quick marinara
- **Frozen peas or spinach** thrown into the pasta water for the last 2 minutes
- **A tin of tuna** or some chopped bacon added to the garlic pan
- **A fried egg on top**, runny yolk mixed in, makes it feel like a proper meal

The main thing to remember: pasta water, cheese off the heat, and more black pepper than you think you need.
