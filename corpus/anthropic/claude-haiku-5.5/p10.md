### Garlicky tomato spaghetti

Serves 2, about 25 minutes. Most of this is probably in your cupboard already.

**You'll need**
- 200 g (7 oz) spaghetti, or any pasta you have
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp chili flakes (more if you like it hot)
- 1 can (400 g / 14 oz) chopped or crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano or Italian seasoning, if you have it
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional: 1 tbsp butter, a handful of grated Parmesan or any hard cheese, and some chopped parsley

**Method**
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it generously. Add the pasta.
2. While it cooks, warm the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until pale gold. Don't let it brown, or it turns bitter.
3. Pour in the tomatoes and add the oregano, a pinch of salt and plenty of black pepper. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, mashing any big pieces with a spoon, until it thickens a little.
4. When the pasta is 1 minute short of done, scoop out ½ cup of the cooking water, then drain. Add the pasta to the sauce with a splash of the water and toss over medium heat for about a minute, until the sauce clings to it. Add more water a splash at a time if it looks dry.
5. Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the butter and cheese if using, taste, adjust the salt, and top with parsley.

**No canned tomatoes?** Make aglio e olio instead. Cook the garlic and chili in 4 tbsp of oil until golden, then toss with the drained pasta, a generous splash of cooking water, lots of black pepper and some grated cheese. It takes about 15 minutes.
