- **Set a minimum you'll always do.** Pick something you can manage on your worst day, like 10 minutes or a short walk. Most days you'll do more once you start, and on tough days the minimum keeps the habit alive.
- **Don't try to make up missed sessions.** Cramming in a long workout after a miss often leads to skipping the next one too. Just go back to your normal schedule.
- **Make starting easier.** Lay out your clothes the night before, keep a bag packed, or put your gear where you'll see it. Friction is one of the biggest reasons people skip.
- **Plan for obstacles.** An "if-then" plan helps, like "If I'm tired after work, I'll change clothes and do 10 minutes anyway." Deciding ahead of time means you're not negotiating with yourself in the moment.
- **Look at why you skip.** If it's the same reason every time, like the workouts are too long, too boring, or at the wrong time of day, change the plan instead of relying on willpower. Picking something you actually enjoy, or booking a class or a workout buddy, can help a lot.

Also, try to treat a missed workout as information, not a verdict on you. One skipped session doesn't undo your progress, and what you do next matters most.
