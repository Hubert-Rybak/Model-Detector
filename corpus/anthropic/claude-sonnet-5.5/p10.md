Here's a reliable one: **garlicky tomato spaghetti**. It takes about 20 minutes and runs on pantry staples.

**Ingredients (serves 2-3)**
- 8 oz (225 g) spaghetti, or whatever pasta you have
- 3 tbsp olive oil (butter works too)
- 3-4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) crushed or diced tomatoes
- Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)
- Salt and pepper
- Parmesan or any hard cheese, for serving

**Steps**
1. Boil a big pot of well-salted water. Cook the pasta about a minute less than the package says. Scoop out a mugful of pasta water before draining.
2. While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and pepper flakes and cook for 1-2 minutes, until fragrant and barely golden. Don't let it brown, or it turns bitter.
3. Pour in the tomatoes and add a good pinch of salt. Simmer for 8-10 minutes, until slightly thickened.
4. Add the drained pasta to the skillet with a splash of pasta water. Toss over the heat for about a minute, until the sauce clings and looks glossy. Add more pasta water if it seems tight.
5. Finish with black pepper, a drizzle of olive oil or a small knob of butter, and plenty of cheese.

**Easy swaps and add-ins**
- **No canned tomatoes?** Skip step 3. Toss the pasta in the garlic oil with a generous splash of pasta water and lots of parmesan. That's basically aglio e olio.
- **Want it heartier?** Stir in a can of tuna, frozen peas, leftover chicken, or a handful of spinach. A fried egg on top works too.
- **Want more depth?** Cook a spoonful of tomato paste with the garlic, or add dried oregano.
- **Want it brighter?** Squeeze in a little lemon at the end.
